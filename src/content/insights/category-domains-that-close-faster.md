High-profile domain sales share a pattern: **clarity beats mystery**.

## What speeds up closings

1. **Visible asking price** with Make Offer fallback
2. **Escrow-ready** messaging above the fold
3. **Single point of contact** for diligence
4. **Content proof** — valuation guides, comps, and platform thesis

## What slows deals

Ambiguous “inquire for price” walls, broken mobile CTAs, and missing structured data that prevents rich results for product listings.

## Applying the pattern here

WeGotBetterData.com now surfaces price, Buy Now / Make Offer / Contact Agent paths, trust signals, and insight content designed for both humans and search engines. That is the conversion stack modern domain marketplaces expect.
