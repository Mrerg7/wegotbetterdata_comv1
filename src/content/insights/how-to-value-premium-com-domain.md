Premium domain pricing is not astrology. Serious buyers triangulate **comparables**, **commercial intent**, and **brand leverage**.

## Start with market comps

Look at recent sales in adjacent categories — AI, data, trust, and B2B SaaS brandables. Public reports on names like AI.com set the ceiling for category ownership, while six-figure .com transactions establish a working mid-market for precise commercial phrases.

## Score commercial intent

Ask:

1. Does the domain answer a question buyers already ask?
2. Can it rank for long-tail queries like “premium domain names” and “[domain].com for sale”?
3. Will it reduce paid acquisition costs by improving brand recall?

## Apply a strategic premium

Category-owning phrases that compress a market thesis — such as **WeGotBetterData.com** — command premiums because they function as both brand and positioning statement. When the name is the pitch, valuation includes future platform optionality, not just traffic.

## Closing checklist

- Confirm clean title and WHOIS control
- Use escrow for settlement
- Document transfer timelines (typically 1–3 business days of handling)
- Align asking price with a clear Make Offer / Buy Now path
