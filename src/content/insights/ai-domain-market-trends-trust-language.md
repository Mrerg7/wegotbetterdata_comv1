The AI naming cycle has shifted. Early winners leaned on novelty and model jargon. The next wave belongs to **trust language**.

## From capability to credibility

Enterprises no longer ask only which model is smartest. They ask whose data can be trusted. Domains that encode that question — accuracy, reliability, “better data” — map directly onto procurement conversations.

## What investors are buying

- Exact-match commercial phrases on premium TLDs
- Brandables that survive product pivots
- Assets that support content marketing and organic acquisition
